*Last updated: 25 October 2026*

This page explains what we collect when you use naturalschoolwear.com.au, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

- **Waitlist:** your email address, and if you choose to give them, your first name, whether you're a parent or from a school, and your school or suburb.
- **Contact form:** your name, email address, the topic and your message.

We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

## How we use it

We use your details to tell you when our uniforms are ready to order, to send occasional news and new articles, and to reply to your messages. Your school or suburb helps us decide where to start.

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. You can also email us and we'll remove you.

## Who processes it

Form submissions are handled by [Formspree](https://formspree.io), and the site is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com). These services may store data outside Australia. They process it on our behalf and only for the purposes above.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing [hello@naturalschoolwear.com.au](mailto:hello@naturalschoolwear.com.au).

## Questions

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, email us first and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also contact the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au).
